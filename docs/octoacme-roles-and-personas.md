# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists design and execute test strategies to validate product quality and acceptance criteria. They partner with developers and product leaders to ensure features are ready for release and meet the agreed definition of done.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned to acceptance criteria
- Execute functional, regression, and release-readiness testing
- Identify, document, and triage defects
- Validate bug fixes and verify readiness for deployment
- Collaborate with developers to improve automated testing coverage
- Participate in release evaluations and quality gate reviews

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality expectations before release
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact
- Increase confidence in deployment readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Test status reports and defect triage meetings
- Release readiness checkpoints and escalation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate fixes, identify regressions, and confirm implementation quality.
- Partners with Product Managers to verify that requirements and acceptance criteria are testable and complete.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release timing, quality risks, and stakeholder communication.

---

## Technical Architect / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Technical Leads provide technical direction for system design, architecture decisions, and engineering standards. They help teams make sound technical choices while balancing scalability, maintainability, and delivery speed.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide technical designs and architecture proposals
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and constraints
- Mentor developers and support technical decision-making
- Ensure systems remain maintainable, secure, and aligned with standards
- Lead architectural discussions and support cross-team design alignment

### Goals
- Maintain system health and scalability
- Reduce technical debt and design drift
- Support fast, safe delivery with clear engineering direction

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Code review feedback and standards alignment
- Risk discussions with engineering and project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers by clarifying technical constraints, design trade-offs, and coding standards.
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, dependencies, and technical risks that affect scope or timeline.
- Helps ensure that delivery choices remain consistent with the broader technology strategy and operational needs.

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic product oversight, guide product direction, and support Product Managers in making informed prioritization and execution decisions. They connect product work to business goals and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Mentor and guide Product Managers on strategy and prioritization
- Review roadmaps for alignment to business objectives
- Approve major product decisions and cross-functional trade-offs
- Manage stakeholder engagement at a strategic level
- Support project initiation and major decision gates

### Goals
- Align product decisions with business strategy
- Strengthen product leadership and execution quality
- Maintain confidence among stakeholders and executive sponsors

### Typical Communication
- PM syncs and roadmap reviews
- Executive briefings and strategic alignment meetings
- Decision-making and initiation review sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides strategic direction to Product Managers and helps resolve competing priorities.
- Collaborates with Project Managers to ensure roadmap commitments are realistic and aligned to delivery capacity.
- Works with Developers and Technical Leadership to assess trade-offs between business value, technical complexity, and schedule risk.

---

## Security & Incident Response

### Role Summary
Security and Incident Response roles ensure security requirements are built into the product lifecycle and help respond effectively when issues or vulnerabilities arise. They act as guardians of secure delivery and operational resilience.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Support security scanning, testing, and CI/CD controls
- Participate in threat analysis and risk assessments
- Lead or support incident response for security-related issues
- Provide security guidance and relevant training to delivery teams

### Goals
- Prevent vulnerabilities from reaching production
- Respond quickly and effectively to security incidents
- Maintain compliance, data protection, and operational trust

### Typical Communication
- Planning reviews and security checkpoints
- CI/CD security gate notifications and issue escalation
- Incident coordination and post-incident review meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Reviews work with Developers to validate secure implementation and reduce risk before release.
- Coordinates with Product Managers and Project Managers on risk prioritization, compliance needs, and release decisions.
- Works with technical leaders to address systemic security risks and maintain secure architecture patterns.

---

## Sponsor

### Role Summary
A Sponsor is an executive or senior stakeholder who champions the project, authorizes its direction, and removes organizational barriers to success. They help keep the initiative aligned with strategic goals and leadership priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and charter
- Allocate resources and support issue escalation
- Help resolve organizational blockers and dependencies
- Communicate the strategic importance of the initiative
- Approve major scope, timeline, or budget changes

### Goals
- Maintain strategic alignment and executive visibility
- Remove obstacles to delivery and decision-making
- Support the successful outcomes of high-impact work

### Typical Communication
- Project approval gates and executive reviews
- Escalation resolution and leadership briefings
- High-level status updates and strategic check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers and Product Leads to align delivery to strategic goals and executive expectations.
- Provides direction to Product Managers and technical leadership when priorities or trade-offs require sponsorship.
- Supports resolving organizational barriers that are beyond the team’s control.

---

## Stakeholder (General)

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are individuals or groups impacted by or invested in the project’s outcomes. They may include customers, internal teams, support staff, dependent engineering groups, or business partners.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on requirements, constraints, and success criteria
- Review deliverables and approve solutions where appropriate
- Share operational or business context that affects scope and readiness
- Participate in planning and review sessions as needed

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes meet their needs and constraints
- Reduce surprises and improve alignment
- Support adoption, usability, and business readiness

### Typical Communication
- Planning and review sessions
- Status updates and acceptance feedback
- Change and dependency discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Contributes requirements and feedback to Product Managers and Developers during planning and validation.
- Works with Project Managers to stay informed on milestone, risk, and delivery progress.
- Provides practical input on usability, operational readiness, and downstream impacts of the solution.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These personas complement the existing Developer, Product Manager, and Project Manager roles by clarifying coverage for QA, architecture, security, sponsorship, and stakeholder coordination.
